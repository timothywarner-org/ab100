# Introduction to agentic AI business solutions

Learn to align AI solutions with business goals and scale AI adoption using Microsoft technologies.

- Module type: Module (8 units)
- Level: Advanced
- Role: Solution Architect
- Products: Microsoft 365 Copilot, Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat, Microsoft Power Platform,
  Microsoft Copilot Studio, Dynamics 365

## Learning objectives

After completing this module, you will be able to:

- Explain the architect's role in driving AI adoption and transformation.
- Identify key responsibilities of an AI architect in business contexts.
- Understand how architects align AI solutions with organizational goals.
- Apply best practices for scaling AI across enterprise environments.
- Identify core Microsoft AI services and tools.
- Explore Microsoft Copilot solutions and their business value.
- Understand how generative AI unlocks productivity in enterprise environments.
- Identify out-of-the-box Microsoft AI agent resources available for business solutions.

## Prerequisites

- Basic understanding of AI concepts.
- Familiarity with enterprise IT environments.
- Awareness of business strategy and organizational goals.

## Units

1. Introduction.
2. Drive AI transformation with architect strategies.
3. Explore Microsoft AI technologies for business.
4. Identify Microsoft AI technologies for business solutions.
5. Identify out-of-box Microsoft AI agent resources for business solutions.
6. Identify out-of-box Microsoft AI agents for business.
7. Module assessment.
8. Summary.

## AB-100 mapping

This module aligns to the **Plan AI-powered business solutions** domain (25-30%). It seeds the
"Plan an architecture for AI-powered business solutions" and "Identify Microsoft technologies and tools that
support AI-powered business solutions" objectives, and orients learners to the architect's framing role
before deeper Plan-domain work in modules 2 through 4.
