# Evaluate costs and benefits of AI solutions

Learn to assess costs, benefits, and ROI of AI solutions while deciding whether to build, buy, or extend AI
components for business processes.

- Module type: Module (7 units)
- Level: Advanced
- Role: Solution Architect
- Products: Microsoft 365 Copilot, Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat, Microsoft Power Platform,
  Microsoft Copilot Studio, Dynamics 365

## Learning objectives

After completing this module, you will be able to:

- Define ROI criteria and evaluate total cost of ownership (TCO) for AI solutions.
- Create a comprehensive ROI analysis for AI-powered business processes.
- Analyze and decide whether to build, buy, or extend AI components.
- Implement model routing strategies to optimize AI performance and cost.

## Prerequisites

- Familiarity with AI concepts and business processes.
- Basic understanding of ROI and TCO principles.

## Units

1. Introduction.
2. Evaluate ROI criteria for AI-powered solutions.
3. Create ROI analysis for a proposed AI solution.
4. Analyze whether to build, buy, or extend AI components.
5. Implement a model router to intelligently route requests to the most suitable model.
6. Module assessment.
7. Summary.

## AB-100 mapping

This module aligns to the **Plan AI-powered business solutions** domain (25-30%). It directly maps to
"Evaluate the costs and benefits of an AI-powered business solution" including ROI, TCO, and the
build-buy-extend decision, and to "Implement a model router" as a cost and performance lever. The
build-buy-extend decision is a recurring AB-100 exam scenario.
