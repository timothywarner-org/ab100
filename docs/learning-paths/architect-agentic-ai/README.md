# Architect AI solutions for business productivity

Discover how to design and implement AI-powered business solutions using agents, generative AI, and Microsoft
Copilot. Gain the skills to analyze requirements and create innovative solutions that enhance productivity
and efficiency.

## At a glance

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Learning Path |
| Level | Advanced |
| Role | Solution Architect |
| Subjects | Generative AI, Data analytics, Data management, Data engineering, Data integration |
| Modules | 11 |
| Products | Microsoft 365 Copilot, Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat, Microsoft Power Platform, Microsoft Copilot Studio, Dynamics 365 |

## Prerequisites

- Basic understanding of AI concepts.
- Familiarity with business process analysis.
- Knowledge of Microsoft tools and platforms.

## Modules in this learning path

1. **Introduction to agentic AI business solutions** -- align AI solutions with business goals and scale AI
   adoption using Microsoft technologies.
   ([local](./01-introduction-agentic-ai.md) |
   [MS Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/training/modules/introduction-agentic-ai-business-solution-architecture/))
2. **Analyze requirements for AI-powered business solutions** -- analyze, design, and implement AI-powered
   business solutions using agents, generative AI, and Microsoft Copilot for productivity.
   ([local](./02-analyze-requirements.md) |
   [MS Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/training/modules/analyze-requirements-ai-powered-business-solutions/))
3. **Design overall AI strategy for business solutions** -- design an enterprise AI strategy using the Cloud
   Adoption Framework, aligning AI agent lifecycle with business goals for operational excellence.
   ([local](./03-design-overall-ai-strategy.md) |
   [MS Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/training/modules/design-overall-ai-strategy-business-solutions/))
4. **Evaluate costs and benefits of AI solutions** -- assess costs, benefits, and ROI of AI solutions while
   deciding whether to build, buy, or extend AI components for business processes.
   ([local](./04-evaluate-costs-benefits.md) |
   [MS Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/training/modules/evaluate-costs-benefits-ai-powered-business-solution/))
5. **Design AI agents for business solutions** -- design AI agents tailored to business needs using
   Dynamics 365 and Copilot.
   ([local](./05-design-ai-agents.md) |
   [MS Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/training/modules/design-ai-agents-business-solutions/))
6. **Design extensibility of AI solutions** -- design scalable, secure, and customizable AI solutions using
   Microsoft platforms.
   ([local](./06-design-extensibility.md) |
   [MS Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/training/modules/design-extensibility-ai-solutions/))
7. **Orchestrate configuration of prebuilt agents and apps** -- orchestrate, configure, and extend AI-driven
   experiences in Dynamics 365 and Microsoft 365 Copilot.
   ([local](./07-orchestrate-prebuilt-agents.md) |
   [MS Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/training/modules/orchestrate-configuration-prebuilt-agents-apps/))
8. **Monitor, analyze, and tune AI agents** -- ensure AI agents operate reliably, deliver high-quality
   outcomes, and continuously improve in enterprise environments.
   ([local](./08-monitor-analyze-tune.md) |
   [MS Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/training/modules/analyze-monitor-tune-ai-powered-business-solutions/))
9. **Manage testing AI-powered business solutions** -- validate and maintain AI-powered business solutions
   with structured testing frameworks, metrics, and governance.
   ([local](./09-manage-testing.md) |
   [MS Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/training/modules/manage-testing-ai-powered-business-solutions/))
10. **Design ALM process for AI-powered business solutions** -- design ALM processes for AI solutions,
    ensuring governance, security, and consistency across environments.
    ([local](./10-design-alm-process.md) |
    [MS Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/training/modules/design-alm-process-ai-powered-business-solutions/))
11. **Design responsible AI security, governance, risk management, and compliance** -- design secure,
    governed, and compliant AI systems that align with organizational policies and Responsible AI principles.
    ([local](./11-responsible-ai-governance.md) |
    [MS Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/training/modules/design-responsible-ai-security-governance-risk-management-compliance/))
