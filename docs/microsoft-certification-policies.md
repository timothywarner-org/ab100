# Microsoft Certification Policies and FAQ

> Last synced: 2026-04-21
> This is a convenience aggregation for learners preparing for Microsoft
> exam **AB-100: Agentic AI Business Solutions Architect**. Official
> Microsoft policy pages always win. If anything here conflicts with
> `learn.microsoft.com`, trust Microsoft.

This reference summarizes what a Microsoft certification candidate agrees to
when they register for and sit an exam such as AB-100. It covers the
candidate agreement (the thing you click-through on exam day), the code of
conduct, retake and reschedule rules, score reports, digital badges, renewal,
accommodations, exam security and proctoring, pricing and discount offers,
and the most common FAQs.

## Candidate agreement and NDA

When you launch any Microsoft certification exam you must accept the
**Microsoft Certification Exam -- Candidate Agreement**. This is the
non-disclosure agreement (NDA) and rules-of-engagement for the exam.

Key clauses, summarized:

1. **Confidentiality.** You must not distribute, copy, display, publish,
   summarize, photograph, record, download, transmit, or post any exam,
   exam tasks, questions, answers, diagrams, or other exam content. Exam
   content is Microsoft confidential intellectual property.
2. **Misconduct prohibited.** You must not misrepresent your identity or
   country of residence, modify exam scores or records, submit work that
   is not your own, use Artificial Intelligence or related products to
   assist you, provide or receive improper assistance, use braindump
   material, possess non-authorized items (phones, watches, notes) during
   the exam, or attempt to reverse engineer the exam.
3. **Privacy and data sharing consent.** You consent that Microsoft, its
   affiliates, and the exam provider (Pearson VUE or Certiport) may share
   your personal information and exam results between each other, with the
   Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program (formerly Microsoft Partner Network)
   for partner compliance checks, with voucher issuers (employers,
   academic institutions, government agencies), and with standards bodies
   affiliated with those issuers.
4. **Tenants.** If the exam includes a lab, any cloud tenant provisioned
   for the lab is Microsoft property, is trial only, cannot be converted
   to a paid subscription, and cannot be used after the exam ends.
5. **Academic exams.** If you register under academic pricing, you must
   confirm you are a student or faculty at an accredited institution.
6. **Score challenge.** You have 15 days after the exam to challenge a
   score or item.

**Consequences of violation.** Microsoft, at its sole discretion and
without refund, may:

- Invalidate the exam results and revoke any credential earned.
- Prohibit you from taking any future Microsoft credentials.
- Permanently ban you from the Microsoft Credentials Program.
- Refer the case to its Worldwide Learning Security Team.

Source:
[Microsoft Certification Exam -- Candidate Agreement](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/support/certification-exam-candidate-agreement).

There is a separate **Microsoft Certification Renewal Assessment Candidate
Agreement** for the free online renewal assessments and a separate
**Microsoft Applied Skills Lab Assessment Candidate Agreement** for lab
assessments. Both follow the same pattern as the exam agreement. AB-100 is
a role-based certification exam, so the exam agreement is the one you will
see on exam day. Renewal assessment rules apply once you renew AB-100.

Sources:
[Microsoft Certification Renewal Assessment -- Candidate Agreement](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/microsoft-certification-renewal-agreement),
[Microsoft Applied Skills Lab Assessment Candidate Agreement](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/support/appliedskills-program-agreement).

## Code of conduct

Microsoft's **Exam and assessment lab security policy** is the operational
code of conduct. You are expected to pass the exam on your own merit,
without AI, bots, or another person, and to keep exam content confidential.

Prohibited behaviors include:

- Using AI in any way during the exam or assessment lab.
- Using braindump sites, either to prepare or during the exam.
- Proxy testing (another person takes the exam for you).
- Falsifying identity or impersonating another individual.
- Falsifying or altering a score report.
- Copying, publishing, reverse engineering, or translating any exam or
  lab content.
- Looking at notes or another candidate's monitor at a test center.
- Bringing phones, watches, or other non-authorized items into the
  testing room.
- Violating the retake policy.

**Reporting cheating or content leaks.**

- Report a suspected braindump site or leaked content via the
  [Worldwide Learning Certification Security form](https://aka.ms/wwlcertsecurity).
- Report testing center security concerns via the Pearson VUE
  [Test Security contact page](https://www.pearsonvue.com/us/en/contact-us/test-security.html).
- Report broader concerns to
  [Credentials Support](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/support/help).

A candidate banned for misconduct may appeal within 14 calendar days by
emailing **mlsecure@microsoft.com**. Appeals received after that window
are not reviewed. Appeals of a ban for a falsified score report require
the original paper report mailed to the Microsoft Worldwide Learning
Security Team, Redmond, WA.

Source:
[Exam and assessment lab security policy](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/support/exam-and-assessment-lab-security-policies).

## Retake policy

For role-based, specialty, and fundamentals exams (which includes AB-100):

1. **First failure.** Wait **24 hours** before retaking.
2. **Second and subsequent failures.** Wait **14 days** between each
   attempt, up to a maximum of five attempts.
3. **Annual limit.** No more than **five (5) attempts in a 12-month
   period** from the date of your first attempt. After five failed
   attempts you must wait 12 months from that first attempt.
4. **Passed exams.** You cannot retake an exam you have passed unless
   the associated certification has expired.
5. **Cost.** You must pay the full exam fee for each retake; there is no
   refund for a failed attempt.

**Waiver of the waiting period.** You can request a waiver only if you
experienced a Pearson VUE-documented internet connectivity or equipment
failure and have the Pearson VUE case number. No other reason qualifies.
Submit waiver requests through
[Training, Certification, and Program Support](https://trainingsupport.microsoft.com/).

**Exam Replay** (paid bundle). Microsoft sells an **Exam Replay** offer
through Pearson VUE that bundles one exam voucher with one retake. Both
must be scheduled and taken within 12 months of purchase. You cannot
schedule the retake until you have sat and failed the first attempt.

Sources:
[Exam and Applied Skills assessment lab retake policies](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/support/retake-policy),
[Offers -- Exam Replay](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/deals).

## Reschedule and cancel

**Free window.** You may reschedule or cancel at no charge if you act
**at least 24 hours** before your scheduled start time.

**Inside 24 hours.** You forfeit the entire exam fee. If you used a
company-purchased voucher, the voucher is consumed. If you no-show, the
purchasing company may be charged a no-show fee.

**How to reschedule or cancel.** Sign into your Microsoft Learn profile,
open **Credentials > Certifications**, find the scheduled appointment,
and click **Cancel** or **Reschedule**. You will be redirected to the
exam provider (Pearson VUE) to complete the change.

**Scheduling limits.** You can have a maximum of two Microsoft
Certification exams scheduled at a time through Pearson VUE (effective
January 16, 2023). You can schedule no more than 90 days in advance.

Sources:
[Reschedule and cancellation policies](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/support/reschedule-and-cancellation-policy),
[Register and schedule an exam](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/register-schedule-exam).

## Score report

**When scores post.** For most exams you see pass or fail and the
numeric score within minutes of finishing. It can take up to 24 hours
for the result to appear in the Learn profile's Certifications tab.
Beta exams and Certiport-delivered exams may take longer -- beta scores
are released roughly 10 days after the beta period closes.

**Scoring scale.** Technical exams including AB-100 use a scaled score
from 1 to 1,000. **A score of 700 or greater passes.** Because it is a
scaled score, 700 does not equal 70 percent correct; the cut score is
set based on the difficulty of the specific form delivered.

**How to access your score report.** Sign into Learn > Profile >
Credentials > Certifications. Scroll to **Past exams**, locate the
exam, and select **View details on provider site** to open the Pearson
VUE dashboard, then choose **View Scores**.

**Format of the score report.** The report shows a numeric overall
score, pass or fail status, and a bar chart with your performance on
each skill area. Microsoft does not tell you which individual questions
you answered wrong.

**Re-score or re-evaluation.** Microsoft does not re-score entire
exams. If you believe a specific item is technically inaccurate, file
an **item challenge** within **15 calendar days** of the exam using the
[Microsoft Certification item challenge form](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/support/exam-item-challenge-process).
A panel of subject matter experts reviews the item. Reviews take about
two weeks. Final exam results are rarely changed, but if a flawed item
is removed Microsoft may offer a free retake.

**Refunds.** Microsoft does not refund failed exams or missed
appointments.

Sources:
[Exam scoring and score reports](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/exam-scoring-reports),
[Exam item challenge process](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/support/exam-item-challenge-process),
[Exam frequently asked questions](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/frequently-asked-questions).

## Digital badges and credentials

When you pass the required exam(s) for a Microsoft certification you
earn a verifiable digital badge. Microsoft's badging partner is
**Credly** (formerly the Acclaim platform).

- You receive email from `mscert@microsoft.com` or `admin@credly.com`
  with a link to accept the badge in your Credly account. Use the same
  email address Microsoft has on file to claim.
- From Credly you can share the badge to LinkedIn, X, Facebook, and
  email, or embed it in a resume. The badge carries metadata that
  third parties can use to verify authenticity.
- Credly also surfaces active job postings that match your
  certification.
- Microsoft has also integrated badges directly into the
  **Microsoft Learn profile** and transcript, so you no longer need to
  leave Learn to download your certificate.
- The pay-to-print certificate and wallet card, and the old MCP
  Profile on certsmarket.com, have been retired.

**Revoking or hiding a badge.** You manage sharing and visibility in
Credly. In your Learn profile, enabling **Private Mode** hides your
name, achievements, and Q and A contributions and prevents anyone from
viewing your transcript.

Sources:
[How to Claim Your Digital Badge -- and Why](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/posts/how-to-claim-your-digital-badge-and-why),

## Renewal

**Validity period.** Microsoft role-based (associate and expert) and
specialty certifications are valid for **one (1) year** from the date
you complete all requirements to earn the certification. AB-100 is a
role-based expert-level certification, so this one-year validity
applies.

**Fundamentals certifications** (for example, AI-900, AZ-900) do **not**
expire.

**MOS, MTA, MCSA, MCSD, MCSE, MCE** certifications do not expire.

**Applied Skills credentials** do not expire.

**Renewal assessment.** The only way to renew is to pass the **free,
online renewal assessment** on Microsoft Learn. There are no alternative
paths -- you cannot renew by passing a beta exam or by re-taking the
original certification exam.

- The renewal assessment is shorter than the full exam (about 45
  minutes) and focuses on updates since the original skills measured.
- There is **no limit** on renewal assessment attempts, but after the
  second attempt you must wait at least 24 hours between future
  attempts.
- Renewal assessments are available in the same languages as the
  corresponding certification exam.
- Some renewal items are drafted with AI assistance but all are
  reviewed by subject matter experts before publication.

**Renewal window.** The renewal assessment opens **six months before**
your certification expires (expiration dates and times are in UTC). You
cannot renew earlier than six months before expiry. Microsoft
recommends renewing well before expiration to allow for retakes.

**Expired certification.** If you miss the window the certification
expires and you must re-earn it by passing the full required exam(s)
again. Expert-level certifications and their associate-level
prerequisites renew independently -- losing the prerequisite does not
automatically expire the expert-level certification.

**Retiring certifications.** When Microsoft retires a certification,
renewal reminders are suppressed and the renewal window may be
shortened for some candidates. Learners with the retired certification
get a direct email with guidance on how long the renewal assessment
stays open.

Sources:
[Credential expiration policies](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/support/credential-expiration-policy),
[Renew your Microsoft Certification](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/renew-your-microsoft-certification),
[Frequently asked questions about renewal](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/renew-your-microsoft-certification-faq).

## Accommodations

Microsoft approves exam accommodations for permanent and temporary
disabilities, learning differences, and test anxiety. Common approved
accommodations include:

- Extra time (for learning disabilities, ADHD, PTSD, test anxiety, and
  similar conditions).
- Use of assistive technology.
- A personal care assistant, reader, or scribe present during the
  exam.
- Flexibility on home-testing environment security rules if you cannot
  physically rearrange your space.

**How to request.** Submit through **Pearson VUE's Reasonable
Adjustments Request System** at `https://microsoft.onhgcloud.com/`
**before scheduling the exam**. New users must register, activate by
email, submit the request form, and upload supporting documents.

**Documentation.** A formal medical diagnosis is **not required**.
Acceptable evidence includes prior testing, training, or workplace
accommodations; IEP or Section 504 plan; a note from a teacher,
manager, or healthcare professional; or the Microsoft
[WWL Healthcare Provider Form](https://query.prod.cms.rt.microsoft.com/cms/api/am/binary/RE50Gl0).
Microsoft does not ask for the underlying condition.

**Timeline.** Plan for **up to 10 business days** for the disability
experts to review. Allow up to **20 days** total before the exam date
so the accommodation can be scheduled.

**Duration.** Approvals for permanent disabilities are valid
indefinitely. Approvals for temporary disabilities must be
re-submitted after one year. Training accommodations and exam
accommodations are handled separately -- you need a separate exam
accommodation even if training was already accommodated.

**Appeal.** If denied, use **Request an Appeal** in Step 6 of the
request system and upload any additional documentation. Contact
`MSFTExamAccomm@microsoft.com` for additional support.

**Non-English exams.** If the exam is not available in your preferred
language you can request an extra 30 minutes (this is a separate
request, not a disability accommodation).

**Unscheduled breaks.** For all exams except MOS, five extra minutes
are built into the exam specifically for unscheduled breaks; you do
not need an accommodation for these. Longer breaks require the
accommodation process. Security rules still apply during breaks.

Sources:
[Request exam accommodations](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/request-accommodations),
[Available exam accommodations and associated documentation requirements](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/available-exam-accommodations-document-requirements),
[Frequently asked questions about exam accommodations](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/accommodations-faqs),
[Exam duration and exam experience](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/support/exam-duration-exam-experience).

## Exam security and proctoring

**Identification.** All test takers must present **one original,
unexpired, government-issued ID** with name, photo, and signature. The
first and last name on the ID must match the Pearson VUE registration
exactly. Photocopies are not accepted. See the
[Pearson VUE ID Policy](https://home.pearsonvue.com/Policies/1/English).

**Test center rules.** Test center staff enforce Microsoft security
policy: they verify ID, walk the testing area, prevent removal of
content, and cannot answer exam questions. Microsoft can suspend
testing at any center where security is suspect.

**Online proctored (OnVUE) rules.**

- Run the **system test** on the exact computer you will use, from the
  exact location, in advance. If the system test fails the exam will
  fail -- take the exam at a test center instead.
- Prepare a clear photo of your ID; a blurry photo means the proctor
  will not let you start.
- Complete self-check-in, including a room scan. A **greeter** reviews
  your ID and room scan, then places you in the proctor queue.
- You are monitored by webcam and microphone the entire time. Mobile
  devices must be out of arm's reach.
- You must stay in view of the camera. Mumbling, fidgeting, and
  looking away are allowed unless they appear suspicious; proctors
  will warn first, then revoke.
- Without an accommodation you cannot read questions aloud.
- If **any unauthorized person** enters the exam room your exam will
  be cancelled. Children under 13 may be present without an
  accommodation only if no other childcare option is available.
- Use the built-in **OnVUE whiteboard** for notes; physical pen and
  paper or external whiteboards are prohibited.

**Duration and seat time for AB-100.** AB-100 is an associate or
expert-level role-based exam. Microsoft's published durations for this
category are 100 minutes of exam time plus 120 minutes of seat time
(without labs), or 120 minutes exam time plus 140 minutes seat time
(if labs are present). Five minutes for unscheduled breaks are built
in. The exact duration for AB-100 is shown at registration.

**Penalties for a flagged incident.** The proctor will warn and, if
the behavior continues, revoke the exam. Microsoft reviews recorded
sessions when cheating is suspected. Confirmed cheaters are
de-certified, have scores revoked, and are banned from future
exams.

Sources:
[Online proctored exams](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/online-exams-intro),
[About online exams with Pearson VUE](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/online-exams),
[Exam and assessment lab security policy](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/support/exam-and-assessment-lab-security-policies),
[Exam duration and exam experience](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/support/exam-duration-exam-experience),

## Pricing, vouchers, and discounts

**List price (USD).** Associate and expert role-based exams,
including AB-100, typically cost **US$165** before local taxes.
Pricing is set per country and per currency and can change at any
time. See the AB-100 exam page for the current price in your
region.

**Student discount.** Academic pricing is available in most countries
and regions (not India or China). To activate it, open your Learn
profile, run the **interest survey**, and select **Student** as your
career path. Once verified, the discount is applied automatically at
checkout.

**Microsoft Learn for Educators (MSLE).** Faculty can request free or
discounted certification exam vouchers and free practice assessments
via the [MSLE Portal](https://aka.ms/MSLEPort). These vouchers are
non-transferable and redeemable at any Pearson VUE center.

**Academic volume licensing.** Institutions can buy Advanced
Role-Based exam vouchers in class packs of 30 via **Microsoft Volume
Licensing** or the Pearson VUE Microsoft Voucher Store. Certiport
delivers Fundamentals exam vouchers for academic institutions.

**Cloud Skills Challenge vouchers.** Microsoft regularly runs free or
discounted exam voucher sweepstakes tied to events such as
**Microsoft Ignite** and **Microsoft Build**. Recent examples:

- Microsoft Applied Skills Sweepstakes (August -- October 2025):
  15,000 50 percent discount Microsoft Certification vouchers.
- Microsoft Ignite Challenge Sweepstakes 2025: discount vouchers for
  Pearson VUE Microsoft Certification exams (not eligible for GitHub
  Certifications).

These vouchers are one-time use, exam-specific, non-transferable, and
cannot be redeemed for cash.

**Event vouchers.** Microsoft at SXSW EDU 2026 vouchers must be
scheduled and taken on or before July 31, 2026.

**Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program vouchers.** Partners receive
exam vouchers as part of partner benefits. Voucher redemption follows
the standard Pearson VUE flow on checkout. Share of vouchers with
your employer is governed by your employer's policies.

**GitHub Student Developer Pack.** The GitHub Student Developer Pack
does not presently include Microsoft Certification vouchers.
Verified-student discounts come through the Microsoft Learn student
verification flow above, not through GitHub.

**Exam Replay.** A paid package that bundles one Microsoft
Certification exam voucher plus one retake voucher, both redeemable
within 12 months.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: Does AB-100 require a prerequisite certification?**
A: No. The AB-100 study guide lists no required prerequisite exam.
Microsoft recommends several associate-level background areas
(Copilot Studio, Microsoft Foundry, Microsoft Power Platform,
Dynamics 365, and generative AI fundamentals) as preparation, but
none is a gate. See the
[AB-100 study guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/ab-100).

**Q: What is the passing score for AB-100?**
A: 700 on a 1 to 1,000 scaled score. This matches every other
Microsoft technical certification exam. See
[Exam scoring and score reports](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/exam-scoring-reports).

**Q: Can I take AB-100 in a language other than English?**
A: Exams are first released in English. Microsoft generally updates
localized versions **about eight weeks after** the English version
is updated. Check the AB-100 exam details page for the current list
of supported languages. If your preferred language is not offered
you can request **30 extra minutes** during registration.

**Q: How much does AB-100 cost?**
A: US$165 or the local-currency equivalent before taxes, consistent
with other associate and expert role-based exams. Check the AB-100
exam page for your exact region.

**Q: How long do I have to take the exam?**
A: Associate and expert role-based exams without labs get **100
minutes of exam time** and 120 minutes of seat time. Exams with
labs get 120 minutes of exam time and 140 minutes of seat time.
Five minutes for unscheduled breaks is included.

**Q: If I fail AB-100, when can I retake it?**
A: After **24 hours** for the first retake. After failures two
through five you must wait **14 days** between attempts. No more
than **five attempts in a rolling 12-month window**.

**Q: If I pass AB-100, can I retake it for a higher score?**
A: No. Once you have passed you cannot retake the same exam unless
the certification has expired.

**Q: Can I get a refund if I fail or miss my appointment?**
A: No. Microsoft does not refund failed exams or no-shows. If you
cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before the exam, your fee is
preserved.

**Q: What happens if I use AI, ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot, or any other
AI product during the exam?**
A: Your score will be invalidated, the credential can be revoked,
and you may be permanently banned from the Microsoft Credentials
Program. The candidate agreement explicitly prohibits using
Artificial Intelligence or related products during the exam. This
is the single most important rule to remember given the topic of
AB-100.

**Q: What happens if my score is invalidated?**
A: Microsoft may refuse to refund the fee, may revoke any
certification you earned, and may prohibit you from taking future
exams. You can appeal a ban by emailing `mlsecure@microsoft.com`
within 14 calendar days of the notice.

**Q: How long is AB-100 valid and how do I renew it?**
A: One year from the date you earned it. Six months before
expiration a **free online renewal assessment** opens on Microsoft
Learn. Pass it to extend the certification another year. There is
no cost and no attempt limit. See
[Renew your Microsoft Certification](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/renew-your-microsoft-certification).

**Q: Can I take AB-100 at home?**
A: Yes, through Pearson VUE online-proctored delivery. You must run
the system test in advance, present a clear government ID, allow
webcam and microphone monitoring, complete a room scan, and keep
mobile devices out of arm's reach. If your setup fails the system
test, Microsoft recommends a test center instead.

**Q: How do I request accommodations for AB-100?**
A: Submit a request through Pearson VUE's Reasonable Adjustments
Request System at `https://microsoft.onhgcloud.com/` **before you
schedule the exam**. Approvals take up to 10 business days; plan
20 days total before your intended exam date. A formal medical
diagnosis is not required.

**Q: Where will my AB-100 digital badge appear?**
A: On your Microsoft Learn profile transcript and in your Credly
account. From Credly you can share it to LinkedIn, embed it in
email signatures, or search associated job postings.

**Q: How do I challenge a question I think was wrong?**
A: Use the
[Microsoft Certification item challenge form](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/support/exam-item-challenge-process)
within **15 calendar days**. Challenges to the entire exam, the
scoring formula, the format, or beta exams are not accepted.
Reviews take about two weeks and rarely change the outcome.

**Q: How do I register to take AB-100 using an employer or event
voucher?**
A: Start registration from the AB-100 exam page. Sign into your
Learn profile, transfer to Pearson VUE, and on the payment screen
select **Add Voucher or Promo Code**. Employer linkage for
discounts is set up via the exam discount flow (verify by entering
the email your employer or event uses).

**Q: Do I have to use my personal Microsoft account or a work
account?**
A: Microsoft strongly recommends a **personal Microsoft account
(MSA)**. If you register with an organizational (Entra ID)
account and later leave the organization, your exam records will
be lost and unrecoverable.

**Q: Does AB-100 include labs?**
A: The exam details page announces whether labs are included at
registration and again on the exam overview screens. Microsoft does
not publish a static list because labs can be removed temporarily
for Azure outages or bandwidth issues.
