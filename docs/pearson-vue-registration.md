# Pearson VUE Registration for Exam AB-100

> Last synced: 2026-04-21
> Always confirm details on learn.microsoft.com before scheduling -- policies change.

Exam AB-100 (Microsoft Certified: Agentic AI Business Solutions Architect) is
delivered exclusively by **Pearson VUE**. You schedule from your Microsoft Learn
profile; Microsoft hands you off to Pearson VUE for the actual appointment,
payment, and delivery (either online with OnVUE or at a physical test center).
This guide walks through the end-to-end flow, the rules that apply once you are
booked, and who to contact when something goes wrong.

For the authoritative study objectives and prerequisite list, see
[docs/ab100-exam-objectives.md](./ab100-exam-objectives.md) and the
[AB-100 certification page](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/agentic-ai-business-solutions-architect/).

---

## Before you schedule

Do these things first. Missing any of them is the most common reason
registration fails or a candidate gets turned away on exam day.

### 1. Prerequisite certification

AB-100 is an Advanced (expert-tier) certification and requires **at least one**
of the following prerequisite certifications on your Microsoft Learn profile
**before** the AB-100 certification can be issued. Scheduling the exam does not
require the prerequisite, but earning the certification does:

- Microsoft Certified: Dynamics 365 Business Central Developer Associate
- Microsoft Certified: Dynamics 365 Business Central Functional Consultant Associate
- Microsoft Certified: Dynamics 365 Customer Experience Analyst Associate
- Microsoft Certified: Dynamics 365 Customer Service Functional Consultant Associate
- Microsoft Certified: Dynamics 365 Field Service Functional Consultant Associate
- Microsoft Certified: Dynamics 365 Finance Functional Consultant Associate
- Microsoft Certified: Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management Functional Consultant Associate
- Microsoft Certified: Dynamics 365: Finance and Operations Apps Developer Associate
- Microsoft Certified: Power Platform Functional Consultant Associate
- Microsoft Certified: Power Platform Developer Associate
- Microsoft Certified: Power Automate RPA Developer Associate
- Microsoft Certified: Azure AI Engineer Associate

Source:
[AB-100 certification overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/agentic-ai-business-solutions-architect/).

### 2. Microsoft Learn profile

- Sign in to [learn.microsoft.com](https://learn.microsoft.com/) with the
  **personal Microsoft account (MSA)** you want the certification awarded to.
  Microsoft recommends an MSA rather than a work or school account, because
  work accounts can be deactivated when you change employers.
- Confirm your **legal name** on the Learn profile **exactly matches** the
  government-issued ID you will present on exam day. Mismatches block check-in
  with no exception. See
  [Manage your certification profile](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/manage-certification-profile).
- Add your **mobile phone number with country code** to your profile. Pearson
  VUE uses it if the proctor loses connection.

### 3. Valid ID

Pearson VUE requires a current, **government-issued** photo ID. The name on the
ID must match your Learn profile exactly. Legal name-change forms and
ID-renewal forms are **not** accepted for online proctored exams.

For the acceptable-ID list, see Pearson VUE's
[Microsoft Before Test Day](https://home.pearsonvue.com/Clients/Microsoft/Online-proctored.aspx)
section and click *View ID requirements*.

### 4. Scheduling ceiling

You may have a maximum of **two** Microsoft Certification exams scheduled
through Pearson VUE at a time, and you cannot schedule a certification exam
more than **90 days** in advance. Source:
[Register and schedule an exam](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/register-schedule-exam).

## Two delivery options

When you reach the Pearson VUE flow, you pick one of:

### Online-proctored (OnVUE)

OnVUE lets you sit the exam from home or a private office. It is the fastest
path for global candidates and the most common choice, but it is also the
strictest environment.

- **System test** -- run the OnVUE system test on the **same computer, same
  network, same location** you plan to use for the real exam. Many failures
  are caused by running the test on a laptop at home and then sitting the
  exam on a locked-down work machine.
  [OnVUE system test](https://system-test.onvue.com/system_test?customer=pearson_vue&clientcode=MICROSOFT&locale=en_US).
- **Approved workspace** -- walled room, door closed, no other people,
  desk clear of books, notes, sticky notes, pens, and secondary monitors.
  Unplug or turn away any secondary displays before check-in.
- **Webcam and microphone** -- forward-facing, at eye level, head and
  shoulders visible. Laptop built-in cameras are fine if they meet spec.
- **ID photo upload** -- taken with your mobile phone during check-in. Both
  the ID and a headshot are uploaded; a greeter reviews them.
- **Room scan** -- 360-degree scan captured by your mobile phone. The greeter
  reviews the photos before unlocking the exam.
- **Facial comparison** -- Pearson VUE uses facial comparison technology to
  match your face to your ID. If you do not consent, schedule at a test
  center instead.
- **Check-in window** -- check-in opens **30 minutes before** your
  appointment and closes **15 minutes after**. The launch and check-in
  process itself takes about **15 minutes**.
- **Mobile phone** -- place it out of arm's reach but close enough to be
  heard. The proctor uses it as a fallback contact.
- **Unscheduled breaks** -- 5 minutes are built into the exam time for
  unscheduled breaks (applies to AB-100 as a role-based exam). The exam
  clock keeps running during the break, and you cannot return to any
  questions you already viewed.

What is NOT allowed in the room:

- Other people (a child under 13 is the only exception; no one else may
  enter or pass through during the exam).
- Hand-held electronics, watches, wallets, purses, hats, bags, coats,
  additional computers, or pagers.
- Books, notepads, sticky notes, typed notes, pens, markers, or whiteboards.
- Eating, smoking, or chewing gum (a transparent, spill-proof water
  container is allowed without a separate accommodation).
- External whiteboards, external pen and paper. Use the built-in OnVUE
  whiteboard instead.

Sources:
[About online exams with Pearson VUE](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/online-exams)
and
[Pearson VUE OnVUE landing page](https://home.pearsonvue.com/microsoft/onvue).

### Test center

Pick a test center if any of the following apply: you cannot guarantee a
private walled room, your home network is shaky, you do not want to be
recorded, or you prefer a pre-configured machine.

- **Find a center** -- the Pearson VUE booking flow lists nearby test
  centers by postal code after you select *In person at a test center*.
- **What to bring** -- the same government-issued ID you registered with.
  Leave phone, watch, wallet, and bag in the provided locker. The center
  supplies everything else.
- **Arrive early** -- plan for **15 minutes** of check-in before the
  appointment time. Late arrivals may forfeit the appointment.

Sources:
[Register and schedule an exam](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/register-schedule-exam)
and the Pearson VUE booking flow reached from the AB-100 exam page.

You may mix and match delivery methods across multiple exams or retakes;
online and test-center scores are scored identically and the exams are of
equal difficulty. Source:
[Online proctored exam FAQs](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/support/frequently-asked-questions-about-online-proctored-exams).

## Step-by-step registration

1. Sign in to [learn.microsoft.com](https://learn.microsoft.com/) with the
   Microsoft account you want certified.
2. Open the [AB-100 exam page](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/exams/ab-100/)
   or the
   [Agentic AI Business Solutions Architect certification page](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/agentic-ai-business-solutions-architect/).
3. Scroll to the **Schedule exam** section and select **Schedule with Pearson VUE**.
4. If prompted, **sign in** again or **create** a Learn profile. Use a
   personal Microsoft account when possible.
5. If your Learn profile does not have a legal name, you are redirected to
   add one. Enter the name **exactly** as it appears on the ID you will use
   on exam day.
6. Complete the one-time handoff form (address, phone with country code,
   contact email). Microsoft does not store this data; it is transferred
   to Pearson VUE.
7. Select **Submit** to transfer to Pearson VUE.
8. On the Pearson VUE site, choose how to take the exam:
   - **OnVUE online** -- proceed to the OnVUE flow.
   - **In person at a test center** -- proceed to test-center search.
9. Pick your exam **language** and agree to the Pearson VUE and Microsoft
   Certification exam terms.
10. For OnVUE, confirm time zone; for test centers, enter a postal code and
    pick a location.
11. Pick an available **date and time**.
12. Apply a **voucher code** if you have one (enter it in the voucher field,
    not the *Private Access Code* field).
13. Pay the remaining fee with a credit card, or use 100% of a voucher.
14. Review the booking confirmation emailed by Pearson VUE. The appointment
    also appears in your Microsoft Learn profile under **Profile** ->
    **Certifications**.

Sources:
[Register and schedule an exam](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/register-schedule-exam).

## Accommodations

If you use assistive technology, need extra time, need another adult in the
testing area, or need any modification to the exam experience, you must
request accommodations **before** scheduling. Pearson VUE does not apply
accommodations retroactively to an existing appointment.

1. Review the
   [available accommodations and documentation requirements](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/available-exam-accommodations-document-requirements).
2. Visit
   [Pearson VUE's Reasonable Adjustments Request System](https://microsoft.onhgcloud.com/)
   and register as a new user if needed.
3. Submit the request, uploading any supporting documents. A formal
   diagnosis is **not** required. See
   [Request exam accommodations](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/request-accommodations)
   for acceptable documentation (past accommodations, IEP/504, manager
   letter, health-professional letter, and so on).
4. Wait up to **10 business days** for a decision.
5. On approval, Pearson VUE emails instructions for scheduling with the
   accommodation applied. Book only after that email.

If English is not your first language and AB-100 is not offered in your
native language, you can request **30 extra minutes** as an ESL accommodation.
See
[English as a second language](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/available-exam-accommodations-document-requirements#english-as-a-second-language).

For permanent disabilities, the accommodation stays on file **indefinitely**;
you do not need to resubmit for future exams unless you want to change the
accommodation. Temporary disabilities require resubmission after one year.

Escalation if Pearson VUE denies the request or goes quiet: email the
Microsoft Exam Accommodations Support team at
[MSFTExamAccomm@microsoft.com](mailto:MSFTExamAccomm@microsoft.com).

## Cancellation, reschedule, and retake policies

### Reschedule and cancellation

- You must reschedule or cancel at least **24 hours before** your scheduled
  appointment.
- Miss that window and you **forfeit the entire exam fee**. If you used a
  company-purchased voucher, the voucher is forfeited. Companies may be
  charged a no-show fee.
- Do the reschedule or cancel from your Learn profile:
  **Profile** -> **Credentials** -> **Certifications** -> **Reschedule**
  or **Cancel** next to the appointment. Learn redirects you to Pearson
  VUE to finish.

Sources:
[Reschedule and cancellation policies](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/support/reschedule-and-cancellation-policy)
and
[Exam reschedule and cancellation policy](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/exam-reschedule-and-cancellation-policy)
(redirects to the same content).

### Retake policy (applies to AB-100 as a role-based exam)

- **First retake** -- wait **24 hours** after the failed attempt.
- **Subsequent retakes** -- wait **14 days** between each attempt.
- **Annual cap** -- maximum of **five (5) attempts** in any 12-month period,
  measured from the first attempt. After five failures you must wait 12
  months from the first attempt.
- You **cannot** retake an exam you have passed unless the certification
  has expired.
- You must pay the full exam fee for each retake (or apply a valid voucher).

Source:
[Exam retake policy](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/support/retake-policy).

**Waiver of the 24-hour or 14-day wait** -- only granted if you hit an
internet or equipment failure during the exam **and** a Pearson VUE case
number was opened. Request the waiver through
[Credentials Support](https://aka.ms/credentialssupport)
and include the Pearson VUE case number.

### No-show

Failing to show up, and failing to reschedule or cancel at least 24 hours in
advance, forfeits the exam fee and any voucher. There is no partial refund.

## Pricing and vouchers

Microsoft does not publish a single global exam price on the AB-100 exam
page; **Pearson VUE shows the exact price in your local currency during
checkout**, and it varies by country (taxes, regional pricing, currency).
As of April 2026, community-reported AB-100 pricing in the United States
is in the mid-US$100s; verify on the Pearson VUE checkout screen, not
third-party sites.

Common voucher sources:

- **Microsoft Learn promotions** -- Microsoft Build, Ignite, and Applied
  Skills sweepstakes periodically issue free or discounted vouchers. Watch
  [Microsoft Credentials offers](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/deals).
- **Exam Replay** -- paid package that bundles one exam voucher plus one
  retake of the same exam. See
  [Exam Replay offer](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/deals#exam-replay).
- **Microsoft Learning Partners** -- partners often bundle vouchers with
  instructor-led training.
- **Student discounts** -- see
  [Certifications for students](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/student-discounts).

Voucher codes go in the *Voucher* field at Pearson VUE checkout. They are
**not** Private Access Codes; do not enter them in that field or the voucher
will fail.

## Exam day checklist

Use this list 30 minutes before your appointment.

- [ ] Government-issued ID present, name matches Learn profile.
- [ ] Mobile phone charged, within arm's reach during check-in, then out
      of reach during the exam.
- [ ] System test re-run on today's machine, today's network.
- [ ] Browser pop-up blockers disabled.
- [ ] Antivirus exception in place for the OnVUE Secure Browser.
- [ ] Secondary monitors unplugged or turned away.
- [ ] Desk clear (no books, notes, sticky notes, pens, wallet, watch).
- [ ] Door closed, room quiet, no one else in the room.
- [ ] Beverage (if any) is in a transparent, spill-proof container.
- [ ] Pockets emptied, sleeves ready to roll up, long hair ready to
      pull back.
- [ ] Logged in to the OS as a local administrator; background processes
      stopped.
- [ ] Checked in via **learn.microsoft.com -> Profile -> Certifications
      -> Go to the exam**, 30 minutes before appointment time.
- [ ] Accept the Microsoft Certification Exam Candidate Agreement when
      prompted.

## Troubleshooting -- things that go wrong

| Problem | First action | Escalation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| System test fails before exam day | Re-run on the same machine and network you will use; check administrator rights; allow OnVUE in antivirus. Switch to a test-center booking if it still fails. | [Pearson VUE Self-Service Support](https://support.pearsonvue.com/Selfhelp/s/?clientCode=MICROSOFT&language=en_US). |
| Cannot start OnVUE check-in | Close any corporate VPN, run as local administrator, switch off pop-up blockers, try an Incognito / InPrivate window if Learn will not load. | [Pearson VUE Microsoft support](https://home.pearsonvue.com/microsoft/contact) chat. |
| Photo upload fails (ID or room scan) | Retake in brighter light, use the mobile-phone upload link (not webcam) when offered, ensure all 4 corners of the ID are visible. | Stay in the chat with the greeter; they can reset the upload step. |
| Name on ID does not match Learn profile | Fix the legal name in your Learn profile **before** check-in; name change forms are not accepted on exam day. | Open a case at [Credentials Support](https://aka.ms/credentialssupport) to update the legal name if Learn blocks self-service. |
| Lost connection mid-exam | OnVUE attempts to resume automatically; follow the on-screen prompt and log back in. Proctor contacts you by chat, then phone. | Open a Pearson VUE case immediately via [Pearson VUE Microsoft support](https://home.pearsonvue.com/microsoft/contact). Keep the case number -- you need it for any retake-waiver request. |
| Proctor does not respond within 30 minutes of self-check-in | Use Pearson OnVUE support live chat from the OnVUE dashboard. Do not pick up your phone unless the proctor calls. | [Pearson VUE Microsoft support](https://home.pearsonvue.com/microsoft/contact). |
| Exam terminated for suspected rules violation | Stop and let the proctor finish. Do not re-launch. | Microsoft Credentials Support via [aka.ms/credentialssupport](https://aka.ms/credentialssupport). Microsoft, not Pearson VUE, decides whether the exam stands, is rescored, or is invalidated. |
| Score not posted after the exam | Wait up to a few hours; scores normally appear in the Learn profile shortly after you submit. | [Microsoft Credentials Support](https://aka.ms/credentialssupport). |
| Score report wrong or you want to challenge a question | Submit an item challenge within the published window; do **not** paraphrase or quote the question. Follow the [exam item challenge process](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/support/exam-item-challenge-process). | [Credential item challenge form](https://forms.office.com/Pages/ResponsePage.aspx?id=v4j5cvGGr0GRqy180BHbR9SQgdTBB3hHnpJO6XSRH7RURDkwT0FBRjQ5TVpFMjZXMTUzOElISVdTTS4u). |
| Accessibility issue during the exam (missing accommodation, assistive tech blocked) | Notify the proctor in chat immediately; ask for the exam to be paused or terminated with a case number. | Email [MSFTExamAccomm@microsoft.com](mailto:MSFTExamAccomm@microsoft.com) and open a case at [Credentials Support](https://aka.ms/credentialssupport). |
| Retake waiver needed (connectivity or equipment failure) | Get the **Pearson VUE case number** from the failed session. | [Credentials Support](https://aka.ms/credentialssupport) with the case number attached. |
| Voucher code will not apply | Confirm it is in the voucher field, not the Private Access Code field. Confirm the email on your Learn profile matches the email the voucher was issued to. | Pearson VUE MindHub support at [mindhubprohelp@pearson.com](mailto:mindhubprohelp@pearson.com) (1-2 business day response). |

- **Pearson VUE Self-Service Knowledge Base** --
  [support.pearsonvue.com/Selfhelp/s/?clientCode=MICROSOFT](https://support.pearsonvue.com/Selfhelp/s/?clientCode=MICROSOFT&language=en_US).

  [mindhubprohelp@pearson.com](mailto:mindhubprohelp@pearson.com). Office

Expected response times:

- Microsoft Credentials Support: typically **1 business day** for a first
  response during listed hours.
- Pearson VUE chat (Microsoft program): near-real-time during business
  hours in your region.
- Pearson VUE email: usually **1-2 business days**.

Never trust a phone number for Pearson VUE Microsoft support from a search
engine or third-party forum; use the country selector on
[home.pearsonvue.com/microsoft/contact](https://home.pearsonvue.com/microsoft/contact)
