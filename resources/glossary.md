# Glossary of Key Terms

## A

**Agent**: An autonomous AI component that performs tasks, makes decisions, or interacts with users on behalf of an organization.

**Agent2Agent (A2A)**: An open protocol for communication between AI agents, enabling interoperability across platforms.

**ALM (Application Lifecycle Management)**: The process of managing an application from inception through retirement, including development, deployment, and operations.

**Azure AI Foundry**: Microsoft's platform for building, training, and deploying custom AI models at scale.

**Azure AI Services**: A collection of AI services including vision, speech, language, and decision APIs.

**Azure OpenAI Service**: Microsoft's managed service for accessing OpenAI models like GPT-4.

## C

**Chain-of-thought**: A prompting technique where the model is asked to show its reasoning step-by-step.

**CLU (Conversational Language Understanding)**: Azure AI service for intent classification and entity extraction.

**Computer Use**: Capability in Copilot Studio allowing agents to automate tasks in apps and websites through visual understanding.

**Copilot Studio**: Microsoft's platform for building custom conversational AI agents.

## D

**Dataverse**: Microsoft's cloud-based data platform underlying Power Platform and Dynamics 365.

**DLP (Data Loss Prevention)**: Policies that prevent sensitive data from being shared inappropriately.

## E

**Embedding**: A numerical representation of text that captures semantic meaning, used for similarity search.

**Entity**: A piece of information extracted from user input (e.g., date, name, product).

## F

**Fallback**: The behavior when an agent cannot understand or handle a user's request.

**Few-shot Learning**: Providing a model with a few examples to guide its responses.

**Fine-tuning**: Training a pre-trained model on specific data to improve performance for a particular task.

## G

**Grounding**: Connecting AI models to specific data sources to provide accurate, contextual responses.

**Guardrails**: Constraints placed on AI models to prevent harmful or unintended outputs.

## H

**Hallucination**: When an AI model generates false or unsupported information.

## I

**Intent**: The goal or purpose behind a user's input.

## J

**Jailbreaking**: Attempts to bypass an AI model's safety controls.

## K

**Knowledge Base**: A structured repository of information used to ground AI responses.

## L

**LLM (Large Language Model)**: AI models trained on vast text data capable of understanding and generating human language.

## M

**MCP (Model Context Protocol)**: An open standard for connecting AI agents to external tools and data sources.

**MLOps**: Practices for deploying and maintaining machine learning models in production.

**Model Router**: A system that directs requests to the most appropriate AI model based on the task.

## N

**NLP (Natural Language Processing)**: Technology that enables computers to understand human language.

## O

**Orchestration**: Coordinating multiple AI components or agents to work together.

## P

**Prompt Engineering**: The practice of designing effective prompts to get desired outputs from AI models.

**Prompt Injection**: A security attack where malicious instructions are hidden in input to manipulate AI behavior.

## R

**RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)**: Combining retrieval of relevant documents with text generation.

**RBAC (Role-Based Access Control)**: Restricting system access based on user roles.

**Reasoning Mode**: Agent capability for step-by-step analysis of complex problems.

**Responsible AI**: Microsoft's framework for ethical AI development covering fairness, reliability, privacy, inclusiveness, transparency, and accountability.

## S

**Semantic Kernel**: Microsoft's open-source SDK for integrating AI into applications.

**SLM (Small Language Model)**: Smaller, more efficient language models optimized for specific tasks.

**System Prompt**: Instructions that define an AI agent's behavior, personality, and constraints.

## T

**TCO (Total Cost of Ownership)**: Complete cost of a solution including initial and ongoing expenses.

**Telemetry**: Data collected about system performance and usage.

**Topic**: A conversation unit in Copilot Studio that handles a specific user intent.

**Trigger Phrase**: Words or phrases that activate a specific topic in a conversational agent.

## V

**Vector Search**: Finding similar items by comparing numerical embeddings.

**Voice Mode**: Agent capability for voice-based interactions.

## Z

**Zero-shot**: Asking a model to perform a task without providing examples.
