## Registration Page Content

### Description

**Key Differentiators:**

- Master Microsoft's newest advanced certification AB-100 with complete coverage of all exam objectives while building production-ready multi-agent AI systems
- Design enterprise AI architectures using MCP and A2A protocols across Microsoft 365 Copilot, Copilot Studio, and Azure AI Foundry
- Build secure, scalable solutions with ALM strategies, ROI analysis, and defense against prompt manipulation

The training uses a structured, visual workflow approach, breaking down complex AI orchestration topics into step-by-step, actionable phases to maximize clarity and retention.

**Course Summary:**

Microsoft's AB-100 certification represents the pinnacle of AI architecture expertise, and this intensive 4-hour session prepares you for both the exam and real-world implementation. Whether you're pursuing certification or architecting enterprise AI solutions, you'll master the complete Microsoft agentic AI ecosystem. We cover every exam objective while building production systems: designing multi-agent orchestrations across Microsoft 365 Copilot, Copilot Studio, and Azure AI Foundry using cutting-edge protocols like MCP (Model Context Protocol) and A2A (Agent2Agent). You'll architect solutions with task agents, autonomous agents, and prompt/response agents, understanding exactly when to build, buy, or extend each component.

This training delivers both certification success and practical mastery. Following the AB-100 exam blueprint, we'll implement the Cloud Adoption Framework for AI, design comprehensive ALM processes, secure solutions against vulnerabilities including prompt manipulation, and prove ROI with total cost of ownership analysis. You'll integrate AI across the entire Dynamics 365 suite (finance, supply chain, sales, service), configure Microsoft 365 agents for business scenarios, and build the governance frameworks required for enterprise deployment. Leave with the confidence to pass AB-100 on your first attempt AND the skills to lead your organization's transformation into an AI-forward enterprise using Microsoft's AI Center of Excellence framework.

---

### What You'll Learn and How You Can Apply It

By the end of this live online course, you'll be able to:

- Pass the AB-100 certification exam with comprehensive knowledge of all three skill domains (Planning 25-30%, Designing 25-30%, Deploying 40-45%)
- Architect multi-agent solutions using MCP, A2A protocols, and Computer Use automation across the Microsoft AI platform
- Implement secure ALM processes, responsible AI principles, and governance frameworks required for enterprise AI deployment
- Calculate ROI, design model routers, and orchestrate AI across Dynamics 365 apps for measurable business transformation
